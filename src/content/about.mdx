My work sits at the intersection of systems engineering and emerging AI: I architect the infrastructure that makes things reliable at scale, and increasingly, the orchestration layers that make AI agents useful in production.

## Experience

**SDE 2 — AWS QuickSight AI & DynamoDB** *(Dec 2022 – Present)*

- Architected and developed the orchestration layer for the "Quick Research" AI agent, enabling large-scale distributed research workloads for millions of QuickSight users.
- Owned and revamped the anti-entropy system for DynamoDB Global Tables, reducing worldwide scan latency for 35 PB of data from 2 years down to 1 month.
- Designed and implemented a live, in-place migration of a core service's node communication protocol from unencrypted to encrypted with zero downtime.
